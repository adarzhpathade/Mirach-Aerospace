## 4. Key Target Markets & Sectors

Mirach Aerospace delivers purpose-specific aerial mobility and autonomous drone technology solutions across three primary verticals:
1. **Defence** (Tactical, border surveillance, loiter munitions, intelligence)
2. **Government** (High-altitude logistic supply, disaster response, public infrastructure)
3. **Civil Operations** (Industrial inspection, surveying, environmental observation)

## 5. Engineering & Technical Capabilities

- **Deep-Tech Hardware & Software:** Complete end-to-end proprietary development of flight hardware, onboard flight avionics, and control software from the ground up.
- **Onboard Artificial Intelligence:** Edge AI computing built directly into aircraft systems for autonomous real-time intelligence gathering, target recognition, intruder tracking, and obstacle avoidance.
- **Aircraft Configurations Mastered:**
  - **Fixed-Wing UAVs:** High endurance, long-range reconnaissance, wide-area mapping.
  - **VTOL (Vertical Take-Off & Landing):** Runway-independent operations combining multirotor flexibility with fixed-wing efficiency.
  - **Tailsitters:** Innovative vertical launch and landing transitions into efficient high-speed horizontal cruising.
  - **Helicopters & Multicopters:** Heavy-lift hovering, precision tactical deployment, loiter munition multirotors.
- **Payload Capacity:** From tactical lightweight payloads up to large-class UAS platforms.
- **Specialized Operational Profiles:**
  - High-terrain / high-altitude logistics payloads (e.g., Himalayan and mountainous armed forces supply).
  - Emergency medical transport and critical supply delivery.
  - Live intruder/theft tracking and automated perimeter reconnaissance.
  - Loiter munitions for precision tactical military operations.

## 7. Product Line & Technical Specifications

### 1. Surveillance Tailsitter — Zeus β
- **Category:** Autonomous Tailsitter VTOL UAV
- **Primary Use:** Tactical Surveillance, live reconnaissance, border monitoring
- **Key Specifications:**
  - **Payload Capacity:** Up to `1.5 kg`
  - **Maximum Speed:** Up to `120 km/h`
  - **Flight Endurance:** Up to `60 min`
  - **Operational Range:** Up to `60 km`

### 2. Loiter Munition Multicopter — Bolt
- **Category:** Tactical Loiter Munition / Strike UAV
- **Primary Use:** Target acquisition, precision strike, tactical swarm integration

### 3. Fixed Wing — X777
- **Category:** Long-Range Fixed Wing UAS
- **Primary Use:** Wide-area reconnaissance, high-altitude surveillance, strategic corridor mapping

### 4. VTOL Logistics and UAM — Eagleray
- **Status:** *Coming Soon*
- **Category:** Heavy-lift Logistics & Urban Air Mobility (UAM)
